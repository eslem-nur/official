What Does This Service Include?

There's no need to rebuild your site. Keeping what you already have in order is often enough.

✏️

Content Updates

Changing text, adding a new product or service, updating prices, swapping photos. You write it, I publish it.

🐞

Bug Fixing

Broken links, images that don't display, forms that don't work, boxes that overflow on mobile. I identify, fix and test them.

🛡️

Security & Backups

I check plugin and theme versions that are out of date. I verify whether your backup routine is actually working.

⚡

Speed Improvements

Compressing oversized images, cleaning unnecessary code, cache settings. I measure page load times and share before-and-after results.

Basic SEO Check

Title and description fields, missing alt text, broken redirects. In the monthly report you can see where each page stands.

📊

Monthly Report

What was done that month, what is still pending, which issues came up. A short, clear summary — without burying you in technical jargon.

How We Work Together

First I get to know your site, then we settle into a regular cycle.

01

Site Review

Free, 1–2 days

  • I go through your site from start to finish
  • I list the bugs, slowdowns and risks
  • We establish which platform it runs on
  • I send you a status summary
02

Maintenance Plan

1–2 working days

  • We decide whether to work monthly or hourly
  • We set the monthly hour allowance
  • Response time and priority level are agreed
  • I obtain your written approval
03

Monthly Working Cycle

Regularly each month

  • I queue up your update requests
  • I run security and backup checks behind the scenes
  • If an urgent issue arises, I respond within your package's response time
  • I test changes before pushing them live
04

Report & Handover

End of month

  • I report the work completed and the hours remaining
  • I offer suggestions for the coming month
  • If you don't wish to continue, I hand over all access cleanly
  • I give you every password I used
