What Does This Service Include?

Not just a PDF — a tidy digital system, ready for daily use and ready to write on.

GoodNotes Notebooks

Hyperlinked notebooks with tabs. Page transitions in a single tap, easy searching, trouble-free page insertion.

🗓️

PDF Planners

🎨

Cover Design

Covers in colours and typography suited to your brand. Several cover options are presented, and you choose one.

How We Work Together

Four steps from idea to usable file. No surprises along the way.

01

Needs Analysis

Step 1

  • Fill in the form below
  • Tell me what you'll use the notebook for (study, journal, memorisation...)
  • We settle the page count and number of sections together
  • Share your colour and style preferences
02

Cover & Structure Design

2–4 working days

  • I prepare 2–3 cover options
  • I plan the section structure and tabs
  • I adapt the colour palette to your brand
  • I get your approval, then continue
03

Page Production & Hyperlinking

3–7 working days

  • I prepare every page individually
  • I link the tabs and the table of contents
  • I place the paper types
  • I test it on a tablet — touch sensitivity included
04

Delivery & Setup Support

Final step

  • I deliver the PDF file in an organised manner
  • I explain how to load it into GoodNotes / Notability
  • Minor corrections are free within the first 14 days
  • If you wish, I also provide the source file
