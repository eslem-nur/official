How We Work Together

Four simple steps from brief to delivery. Clear communication throughout.

01

Brief & Discovery

Step 1

  • Fill out the order form below
  • Tell me about your mission and target audience
  • Share any inspirations or references you like
  • Discuss what you DON'T want as much as what you do
02

Concept Development

3–5 working days

  • I create 2–3 initial logo concepts
  • Show them with mockups (business card, website header)
  • We discuss what resonates with you
03

Refinement

Up to 3 rounds

  • Choose your favourite concept
  • We refine colours, spacing and details together
  • No rush — take time to decide
04

Delivery & Support

Final step

  • Receive all files in organised folders
  • Brand guidelines PDF included
  • 14 days of post-delivery support for small tweaks
